Car park plans & design

An accurate, up-to-date equipment plan is the foundation of any car park project. We produce it on demand, from your documents or an on-site survey.

Car park plans on demand

For a new, refurbished or extended car park: layout of payment equipment, entry/exit flows, signage, EV chargers, cameras.

  • Equipment layout plan
  • Power and low-voltage schematics
  • Cable routes and supplies
  • DWG / PDF delivery, equipment schedule

Plan updates

Your car parks evolve: new ticketing, EV chargers, LPR cameras. We update your plans so they reflect the site as it is.

  • Survey of installed equipment
  • Integration of new equipment
  • Version history
  • Plans usable by your maintenance teams

Retrofit studies

Replacing end-of-life ticketing, moving to licence plate recognition, adding EV charging: we assess feasibility and cost.

  • Assessment of existing installations
  • Modernisation scenarios
  • Price-list quotation
  • Site-by-site rollout schedule

Support for local authorities

From plan to commissioning, we support local authorities in their parking policy and solution choices.

  • Needs and usage analysis
  • Technical recommendations
  • Consistency with existing equipment
  • Commissioning follow-up
FAQ
How long does a plan take?

From a few days for a simple update to 2–3 weeks for a full multi-site retrofit study.

Do you work from our existing plans?

Yes. Send us your DWG/PDF files; if none exist, we carry out an on-site survey.

Can you follow on with the installation?

That is our strength: the same team designs, installs and maintains, with no loss of information.

A car park to equip, modernise or maintain?

Reply within 2 working days. Price-list quotation, no commitment.
