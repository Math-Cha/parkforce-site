Maintenance — two options

Your equipment, our technicians

Our teams are trained on each manufacturer's equipment and work at the heart of car parks, in the way that suits you.

Dedicated teams — manufacturers

You build or distribute parking equipment: we provide technicians working 100% for you, embedded in your branches and processes.

  • Vehicle, tools, laptop and phone provided

On-demand maintenance — operators

  • Scheduled preventive visits (monthly, quarterly…)
  • Repairs on every equipment we are trained on
  • Framework contract or one-off job, clear price list
  • Reports and asset tracking after each visit
