03 — MAINTENANCE

Maintenance & operations

Our technicians are trained on each manufacturer's equipment and work directly inside your car parks, as a dedicated team or on demand.

Dedicated teams — manufacturers

Equipment manufacturers and distributors: we provide technicians working 100% for you, embedded in your branches and processes.

  • Technicians trained and certified on your products
  • Vehicle, tools, laptop and phone provided
  • ParkForce management, reporting and service continuity
  • Temporary reinforcement or permanent regional presence

On-demand maintenance — operators

Operators, concession holders, owners: preventive and corrective maintenance on a pre-agreed schedule, on the equipment we are trained on.

  • Scheduled preventive visits (monthly, quarterly…)
  • Corrective repairs and on-call service
  • Framework contract or one-off job, clear price list
  • Report and asset tracking after each visit

Technical scope

Payment and barriers, cameras and LPR, EV chargers, lighting, fire safety, doors, sensors: one partner for every package.

  • Diagnosis and repair
  • Parts replacement
  • Updates and configuration
  • Retrofit and modernisation

Quality and safety

Structured interventions: manufacturer procedures, electrical qualifications, user safety and traceability.

  • Up-to-date qualifications
  • Procedures and checklists
  • Intervention reports and photos
  • Shared performance indicators
